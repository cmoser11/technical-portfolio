# Other analytical campaigns

Shorter notes on other batch work run through the tools in this portfolio. Results belong to my employer and are not described.

- **Housing reform universe.** Estimates for 406 housing-related federal reform ideas, with a summary workbook and reporting on drift between review rounds.
- **Bill fiscal scores.** Estimates for individual congressional bills, delivered as scored reports.
- **End-to-end calibration.** Estimated the effects of rescinding a federal building energy-efficiency rule, run over four rounds to calibrate the platform end to end.
- **Statewide zoning scoring.** Hundreds of Texas jurisdictions scored by [the zoning tool](../projects/03-zoning-ordinance-extraction/), published as a public map.
- **Regulation deep dives.** Agency-level deep dives in [the regulation retrospective pipeline](../projects/06-regulation-retrospective/).
