# Notes on numbers

All figures were measured on 4 November 2026 from the git history and file listings of the repositories I worked in, and are rounded. They describe the volume of my own work. By design, this portfolio gives no figures about the quality or results of the employer's products or analyses.

- **Commits** count every commit attributed to my author identities across all branches of a repository, including merge commits.
- **Files** count Python files on the main branch, excluding archived and legacy folders.
- **Test files** count files following the `test_*.py` naming convention.
- **Tests** count test cases collected by pytest, without running them.
- **Lines of Python** count raw lines in Python files, including tests, comments, and blank lines.
- **Figures from the public tools**, such as the share of DC land the United States owns or the number of Texas municipalities measured, are quoted from those public pages as they appeared on 4 November 2026.
